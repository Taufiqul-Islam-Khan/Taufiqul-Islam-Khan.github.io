Beyond Work

A Little About Me Outside the professional life

Research and teaching take up most of my days, but here's what keeps me human outside of it.

Movies & Series

I'm a big movie person. Thrillers, sci-fi, psychological dramas, anything with a story that makes you think after it ends. I'll happily binge an entire series in a weekend if it hooks me in the first episode.

Music

Music keeps me going during long writing and coding sessions. Mostly Bangla bands, old Hindi classics, and some English indie. No particular loyalty. If it sounds good and fits the mood, it's on the playlist.

Life Philosophy

A few things I genuinely believe in: no work is too small to do well. Respect your parents while you still can. Work smart, but don't be afraid to work hard when it matters. And don't forget to enjoy today, because tomorrow will sort itself out.

New Places, New Perspectives

Moving from Dhaka to Winnipeg was one of the biggest shifts of my life, not just geographically, but in how I see the world. I've come to value the experience of being somewhere completely new, figuring things out from scratch, and meeting people whose lives are nothing like yours.
